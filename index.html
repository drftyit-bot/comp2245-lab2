Days of Campus Future

October 2nd, 2026 by Ethan Watson

After returning from my summer break to continue my tenure at UWI Five Islands, I realize that the months I spent in the first academic year fearing the new environment that I resided in was just a waste of time. I feel as though I'd lived on this compound all my life at this point, you know?

Its good to have a bit of healthy fear in the heart but when you burst out that comfort zone its hard to go back!

Trust your gut. Do your work.

October 4th, 2026 by Ethan Watson

Believing you have more time to do work at any point in time is a false sense of security that you unfortunately are not being more mindful of, or should I only speak for myself? Being a student for more than 16 years of my life I still can't shake the desire to procrastinate.

My worst trait manifests in many corners of my life and I aim to end it somehow, someday. This message is for everybody who is like me: There is no greater time than the present.
